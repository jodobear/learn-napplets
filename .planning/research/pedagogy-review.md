# Pedagogy Review

## Research question

Which reusable learning-product patterns can be proposed from Learn FIPS, MDN Learning, and The Odin Project without treating an unpinned comparator as evidence authority?

## Sources and immutable revisions

All three named candidates are **excluded from comparison evidence**: `CAND-LEARN-FIPS`, `CAND-MDN-LEARNING`, and `CAND-ODIN-PROJECT` are discovery pointers in `candidate-source-manifest.yaml`, each `acquisitionState: blocked`, and none resolves to a `SRC-*` record. Thus none currently supplies the required official URL plus immutable revision/captured version-date, locator, digest, license/use constraints, selection rationale, and evidence state. `SRC-POLICY-001` and `SRC-POLICY-002` define this exclusion policy; they are project-policy records, not comparator proof.

## Observations

The preserved curriculum archive proposes a course with instruments, four synchronized detail modes, reasoning assessments, source-status metadata, static equivalents, and explicit simulation labels. Those are archive/project inputs, not observations of the excluded comparators. Comparator-specific sequencing, instruments, accessibility, content/data separation, and reusable/avoid patterns are unknown.

## Conflicts

There is no pinned comparator evidence to compare. Treating a current marketing/docs page, remembered curriculum pattern, or Graphify relationship as a comparator finding would conflict with the immutable-evidence floor.

## Inference

A safe proposal is to retain the project’s source-linked instrument and assessment shape for later review, while declining any attribution to Learn FIPS, MDN Learning, or The Odin Project. The proposal does not verify pedagogy efficacy or a curriculum scope.

## Prototype or measurement

None. No comparator was captured, licensed, or measured.

## Recommendation

Block comparator-derived recommendations. On acquisition, capture one named official artifact per comparator with immutable/captured identity, locator, SHA-256 digest, license/use constraints, selection rationale, and state; then compare sequencing, instruments, accessibility, content/data separation, and reusable/avoid patterns. Until then, use only the archive’s clearly labeled proposal: source-linked Story/System/Wire/Code views, inspectable/static equivalents, and reasoning assessments.

## Uncertainty

Material. Comparator behavior, accessibility practices, and reuse rights are unverified. `OQ-UPSTREAM-BASELINE-001` is the current common acquisition blocker; comparator-specific review work must be added before any claimed comparison.

## Affected phases and requirements

Phase `01` teaching-scope and lesson research; later Phase 2 content-contract work. Requirements: `EVID-03`, `EVID-04`. Evidence state: `CMP-BASELINE-001`, `CLM-UPSTREAM-BASELINE-001`, `DRF-DISCOVERY-001`.

## Owner and required approval

Owner: research-owner. Required approval: content-learning and protocol-technical human review. Any later curriculum recommendation remains proposed under D-25 through D-28.
