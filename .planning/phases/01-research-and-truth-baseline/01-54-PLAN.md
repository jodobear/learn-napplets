<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries
| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| final plan/source/test bytes → review binding | A historical, altered, partial, or wrong-commit review could authorize terminal execution. |
| candidate/reviewer/verifier identities → independent execution | One principal could reuse an identity to self-certify a candidate. |
| clean worktree command output → independent batch | Missing, duplicate, stale, or substituted evidence could be published as independent verification. |

## STRIDE Threat Register
| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-54-01 | Tampering | final review binding | high | mitigate | Task 1 recomputes complete active-plan and final source/test maps from the immutable reviewed commit. |
| T-54-02 | Spoofing | reviewer/verifier identities | high | mitigate | Tasks 1 and 2 require pairwise distinct nonempty candidate executor, external reviewer, and verifier identities. |
| T-54-03 | Repudiation | external review evidence | high | mitigate | Task 1 binds raw review digest, reviewer method, record commit, convergence record, and candidate digest. |
| T-54-04 | Tampering | independent batch | high | mitigate | Task 2 validates exact command, commit, digest, count, uniqueness, and output bindings before a report is eligible. |
| T-54-05 | Elevation of Privilege | terminal conclusion | high | mitigate | Task 3 leaves the candidate pending external review and retains every no-authorization boundary. |
</threat_model>
