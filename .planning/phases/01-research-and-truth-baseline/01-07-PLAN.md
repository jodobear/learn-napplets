<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| upstream/package/runtime evidence → ecosystem catalog | Labels and examples can overstate compatibility or authority without source linkage. |
| catalog → selected first lab | A weak or disputed candidate could become an unsafe or dishonest later lab. |
| learning comparison → delivery recommendation | Product inspiration must not be confused with protocol or architecture truth. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-07-01 | Spoofing | example/package provenance | high | mitigate | Require immutable revision, public export evidence, license/adaptation status, and source IDs. |
| T-07-02 | Tampering | teaching-scope recommendation | high | mitigate | Link selected-or-blocked outcome to compatibility, drift, source, and open-question IDs. |
| T-07-03 | Elevation of Privilege | teaching-host conclusions | high | mitigate | Treat profiles as proposed experimental scope; do not create a production host or grant guest authority. |
| T-07-04 | Repudiation | delivery recommendation | medium | mitigate | Separate observations, inference, project recommendation, uncertainty, and later human approval. |
| T-07-SC | Tampering | package evidence | high | mitigate | Catalog only public released surfaces and defer any installation to a spike-local human checkpoint. |
</threat_model>
