## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-11-04 | Tampering | browser conclusion | medium | mitigate | Separate upstream source claims, browser observation, and proposed policy in the report. |
| T-11-SC | Tampering | Playwright/browser runtime | high | mitigate | Reuse Plan 01’s approved browser toolchain and record executable/version/flags. |
