<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| research records → learning packet | Source maturity and uncertainty can be lost when technical evidence becomes pedagogy. |
| candidate instrument → future lab/content | Simulated or observed behavior can be misrepresented as real protocol behavior. |
| current work → contribution instruction | Directional upstream activity can be mistaken for supported contributor guidance. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-08-01 | Tampering | lesson evidence links | high | mitigate | Require existing stable claim/source/drift/open-question IDs and common validation. |
| T-08-02 | Spoofing | instrument provenance | high | mitigate | Label each candidate as real boundary, deterministic fixture, conceptual simulation, or implementation observation. |
| T-08-03 | Information Disclosure | teaching examples | medium | mitigate | Require deterministic fixtures and prohibit real secrets, production signing, uploads, and live required paths. |
| T-08-04 | Repudiation | uncertainty disclosure | medium | mitigate | Every packet includes an explicit do-not-teach-as-settled section and follow-up research. |
| T-08-SC | Tampering | generated packet inputs | high | mitigate | Consume only validated canonical research records and preserve their current status. |
</threat_model>
