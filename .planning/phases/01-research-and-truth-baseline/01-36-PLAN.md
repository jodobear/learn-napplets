## Scope-sanity rationale

This is one bounded SPK-G trust-boundary unit: recovered canonical eligibility and dated approval are consumed by one OS-sandboxed package-operation gate, whose exact operation or no-operation blocker must be retained as digest-bound spike evidence and an impact hand-off. Task 1 owns every executable reader/operation path, its recovery registration, sandbox contract, and the source-bound operation contract; Task 2 owns the three retained evidence outputs generated from that same checked runner. Their task-local recovery, eligibility, sandbox, and five-run/validator commands verify the boundary at the transition where untrusted package behavior could otherwise be overstated.

Separating report retention from the reader and runner would weaken atomic verification because a retained result could no longer be demonstrated as the output of the exact recovery-guarded, approval-gated sandbox operation; separating the executable paths would duplicate the recovery and package-argv authority handoff. Plan 01-36 remains exactly two tasks with explicit ownership of its ten paths. Plan 01-36 is the sole Wave 6 owner. Dependent Plan 01-43 moves to Wave 7 and consumes this plan's reviewed SPK-G result; Plan 01-36's shared recovery file is serialized by its declared dependency on earlier Plan 01-42.
