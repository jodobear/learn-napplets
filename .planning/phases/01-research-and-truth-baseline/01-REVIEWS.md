### 1. Plan 01-32 can reintroduce calendar-age invalidation into content-addressed review authorization

**Actionable: yes**

Mechanism: an executor can reasonably implement a timestamp-age threshold in Plan 01-32, making unchanged exact bytes expire. That contradicts Plan 01-29’s acceptance contract and makes authorization dependent on implementation interpretation.

Required correction: state that the timestamp must be syntactically valid and retained for audit, but elapsed age alone cannot invalidate a review. Only byte/commit mismatch or explicit supersession may do so.

### 2. Plan 01-40’s “exactly once” probe contract is unsatisfiable

**Actionable: yes**

Plan 01-40 requires each named probe to execute exactly once (`01-40-PLAN.md:21-40`). Task 1 first runs full `unittest discover`, then directly invokes each of the fourteen tests (`01-40-PLAN.md:128`). Its automated verification runs full discovery again (`01-40-PLAN.md:129`), and Task 2 later runs full discovery once more (`01-40-PLAN.md:148`).

Mechanism: after gap implementation, normal unittest discovery includes the fourteen named tests. Each therefore executes during discovery and again through its dedicated invocation. The dossier’s one-row-per-probe check proves table uniqueness, not execution uniqueness.

Required correction: either:

- redefine the contract as “each probe appears exactly once in the evidence matrix,” while permitting suite reruns; or
- exclude the fourteen probes from discovery and execute them only through one controlled probe runner whose invocation manifest is validated.
