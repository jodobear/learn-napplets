### A1 — Plan 29’s threat register assigns two mitigations to the wrong tasks

Severity: MEDIUM.

Plan 29’s scope rationale correctly assigns the protected collector and candidate tracer to Task 2 and the acquisition queue and receipt history to Task 3 (`01-29-PLAN.md:136-151`). Required correction: assign source-byte/cache confinement to Task 2 and acquisition receipt/history retention to Task 3, then rebind the changed plan snapshot.

### A2 — Plan 31 retains obsolete package-work ownership after moving that work to Plan 41
