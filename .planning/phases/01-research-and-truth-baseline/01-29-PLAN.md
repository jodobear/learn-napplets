## Scope-sanity rationale

This is one bounded vertical trust-boundary unit: the exact 14-file set takes a Phase 1 command from a recorded isolated interpreter and independently bound review bytes, through wrapper-enforced dependency integrity, to a read-only immutable-source acquisition receipt and a deliberately blocked-or-reviewable classification. Task 1 owns authorization before any site-enabled command, Task 2 owns the protected toolchain and candidate ingress tracer, and Task 3 retains the corresponding queue and receipt history; its focused automated commands exercise each transition locally and the final collection command proves the complete path.

Splitting acquisition/receipt retention away from bootstrap, review binding, and wrapper verification would either let a collector produce canonical-facing evidence without its authorizing boundary in the same verified slice or require a second plan to reproduce that authority handoff. The two tasks that both modify `tools/phase1-python` intentionally preserve one wrapper boundary within this plan. Plan 01-29 remains exactly three tasks with explicit path ownership, and it is the sole Wave 1 plan, so its 14 paths cannot collide with another same-wave plan.
