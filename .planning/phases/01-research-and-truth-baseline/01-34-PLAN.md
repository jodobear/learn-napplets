## Changeset scope, sequencing, and rollback

The ten listed paths are intentionally one causal migration-to-enforcement chain, not two independent trust-boundary repairs. Task 1 first makes the versioned record contract and its retained lineage valid; Task 2 can safely interpret that stable shape for path/source confinement and observed-local classification only after the Task 1 validation boundary passes.

| Changeset | Files and owned portions | Boundary and rollback |
|---|---|---|
| Migration | `.planning/research/schemas/drift.schema.json`; structural conversion in `.planning/research/drift-register.yaml`; `tools/migrate-phase1-records.py`; `schemas/legacy/drift.schema.v1.json`; both drift fixtures; `migration-notes.md`; migration cases in `tests/phase1/test_drift.py` | Runs first. Its named tests, deterministic temporary conversion, fixture comparison, and current-format validation must pass before enforcement begins. Failure returns only these changes to their pre-task state and produces no downstream publication. |
| CR-05/CR-08 enforcement | `tools/validate-research.py`; `tests/phase1/test_spike_consolidation.py`; confinement/classification cases in the shared `tests/phase1/test_drift.py`; classification amendments in the migrated `drift-register.yaml` | Runs second from the verified migration boundary. Failure restores its validator, test, and classification portions to that boundary while retaining the valid migration; it produces no Plan 01-42 publication or downstream handoff. |

The two shared paths are intentional: `drift-register.yaml` is migrated before its local observations can be accurately classified, and `test_drift.py` must prove both the retained migration semantics and the post-migration classification semantics. The intermediate boundary prevents either responsibility from masking failure in the other, while the no-publication rule prevents a consumer from observing a migration-only or partially enforced record.
